## Can I use it for free?

All art below costs nothing. What differs is what the license asks in return:

- **Free, no conditions** - CC0.
- **Free, credit the authors (fine for commercial games, Steam, consoles)** - OGA-BY or CC-BY: show the authors on a Credits screen.
- **Free, credit + share-alike (edits of this art must stay CC-BY-SA/GPL; the DRM clause makes it risky for Steam/consoles)** - only CC-BY-SA or GPL available.
